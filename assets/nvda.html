What the numbers say

Overfitting check. A moving-average parameter sweep on NVDA produced an in-sample versus out-of-sample correlation of +0.031. 32 parameter combinations were fitted on the first 70% of history and graded on the unseen 30%. Best in-sample CAGR was 27.2%; the same selection managed 59.9% on data it had never seen.
Risk note. Volatility targeting cut maximum drawdown to -24.7% versus -85.1% for buy and hold — at the cost of a lower return (15.0% versus 38.9%). Drawdown is what forces people out of a position; returns they never collect are not returns.
