What the numbers say

Overfitting check. A moving-average parameter sweep on BTC-USD produced an in-sample versus out-of-sample correlation of -0.012. 32 parameter combinations were fitted on the first 70% of history and graded on the unseen 30%. Best in-sample CAGR was 57.8%; the same selection managed 20.8% on data it had never seen.
Risk note. Volatility targeting cut maximum drawdown to -22.4% versus -83.4% for buy and hold — at the cost of a lower return (15.0% versus 34.9%). Drawdown is what forces people out of a position; returns they never collect are not returns.
