What the numbers say

Overfitting check. A moving-average parameter sweep on IWM produced an in-sample versus out-of-sample correlation of +0.036. 32 parameter combinations were fitted on the first 70% of history and graded on the unseen 30%. Best in-sample CAGR was 4.9%; the same selection managed 9.1% on data it had never seen.
Risk note. Volatility targeting cut maximum drawdown to -30.3% versus -58.6% for buy and hold — at the cost of a lower return (3.1% versus 8.5%). Drawdown is what forces people out of a position; returns they never collect are not returns.
