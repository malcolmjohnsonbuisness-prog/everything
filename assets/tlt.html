What the numbers say

Overfitting check. A moving-average parameter sweep on TLT produced an in-sample versus out-of-sample correlation of -0.362. 32 parameter combinations were fitted on the first 70% of history and graded on the unseen 30%. Best in-sample CAGR was 6.7%; the same selection managed -1.8% on data it had never seen.
Risk note. Volatility targeting cut maximum drawdown to -51.8% versus -48.4% for buy and hold — at the cost of a lower return (0.5% versus 2.8%). Drawdown is what forces people out of a position; returns they never collect are not returns.
