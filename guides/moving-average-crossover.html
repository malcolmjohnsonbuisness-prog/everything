SMA 50/200 crossover results: the classic trend filter

Original backtest research · 18 assets · 576 parameter sets · 2005–2026

The 50/200 moving-average crossover tested across 18 assets over 20 years. It reduced drawdown versus buy and hold on most assets but gave up return.

The rule

Hold when the 50-day moving average is above the 200-day; stay out otherwise. The most widely known trend filter in retail trading.

Cross-asset record

Mean CAGR was 12.8%, versus 17.8% for buy and hold, while mean maximum drawdown improved to -45.2%.

The overfitting trap. Moving-average windows are the most heavily optimised parameters in retail trading. Across our 576 parameter sets the mean in-sample versus out-of-sample correlation was -0.040 — so the specific window you choose carries little predictive information.
