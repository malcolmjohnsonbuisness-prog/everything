Transaction costs in backtesting: why free trading is a fantasy

Original backtest research · 18 assets · 576 parameter sets · 2005–2026

Backtests that assume zero costs overstate performance, especially for high-turnover rules. We apply 5bps per position change throughout, and costs can turn a marginal winner into a loser.

The hidden assumption

Many published backtests quietly assume trading is free. Every position change in reality carries spread, commission and slippage. A rule that trades weekly can lose a large fraction of its apparent edge before a single real order is placed.

What we apply

Every result on this site charges 5bps per position change. That is a conservative retail assumption, and it is applied to every strategy including buy and hold.

Why it disproportionately hurts active strategies. Buy and hold barely trades. A moving-average crossover can flip dozens of times a year, and each flip pays. Costs therefore fall hardest on precisely the strategies that claim to add value.

Test it yourself

Re-run any strategy with costs at 0bps and at 25bps. If the conclusion flips, the strategy was never really about the signal.
