How to avoid overfitting a trading strategy

Original backtest research · 18 assets · 576 parameter sets · 2005–2026

A practical method for avoiding overfitting: split your data, measure the in-sample versus out-of-sample correlation across your full parameter sweep, and treat an unstable sweep as a rejection.

Step 1: split before you look

Fit on roughly 70% of history and reserve the last 30%. The discipline that matters is never inspecting the reserved portion until the rules are frozen.

Step 2: measure the correlation, not just the winner

The single most informative number is the correlation between in-sample and out-of-sample performance across your whole parameter sweep. If great in-sample results do not predict great out-of-sample results, your search was noise. Our cross-asset measurement was -0.040.

Step 3: demand stability

A strategy whose neighbouring parameters all perform similarly is more trustworthy than one whose performance collapses when a single parameter moves. Fragile peaks are the signature of overfitting.

Step 4: include costs and remove lookahead

Shift every signal by one day and charge yourself 5bps per position change. Many rules that appear profitable evaporate under both.
