Why the drawdown column matters more than the return column

The highest-returning assets also carried the deepest drawdowns: Ethereum −94.0%, Nvidia −85.1%, Bitcoin −83.4%. A return figure you cannot sit through is not a return you receive. This is why comparing Sharpe and Calmar — not headline CAGR — is the difference between a strategy you can hold and one you abandon at the worst moment.

Common questions

Do trend-following strategies beat buy and hold?

Rarely on return. The 50/200 crossover averaged 12.8% versus 17.8% for buy and hold.

What reduces drawdown best?

Volatility targeting, by 35.1 percentage points on average.

How were costs handled?

5bps per position change on every trade, throughout.
